# Legions of Nagash [LEGENDS]

## Army Rules

### Battle Traits

#### Ethereal (Passive)
- **Effect:** Ignore negative modifiers to save rolls for friendly Legions of Nagash Nighthaunt units, excluding **Nagash**.

#### Legions of Endless Dead (Passive)
- **Effect:** Subtract 5 from the control scores of enemy units while they are in combat with 2 or more friendly units if any of those friendly units is **Nagash** or if none of those friendly units share any of the following keywords: Soulblight Gravelords, Flesh-eater Courts, Nighthaunt or Ossiarch Bonereapers.

### Artefacts of Power

#### Soul-shrive Amulet (Activated)
- **Timing:** Once Per Battle, Any Hero Phase
- **Declare:** Pick **empowered agent** or **sacrificial pawn**.
- **Effect:** If you picked **empowered agent**, for the rest of the turn:
• Add 2 to the Attacks characteristic of this unit's melee weapons.
• Add 1 to the Rend characteristic of this unit's melee weapons.
If you picked **sacrificial pawn**, instead of making a casting roll for the next spell cast by the friendly **Nagash** this turn, you can use a value of 7 for the roll that cannot be modified and that spell cannot be unbound. Then, allocate 3 damage points to this unit (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points).

### Heroic Traits

#### Inescapable Fate (Activated)
- **Timing:** Once Per Battle, End of Any Turn
- **Declare:** This unit can only use this ability if it has been destroyed.
- **Effect:** If the friendly **Nagash** is on the battlefield, set up a replacement of this unit wholly within 12" of that **Nagash** and more than 9" from all enemy units. If this unit is not Infantry or Cavalry, allocate 8 damage points to it (ward rolls cannot be made for those damage points).
