### Iron Father Feirros
**Points:** 95 | **M:** 5" | **T:** 6 | **SV:** 2+ | **W:** 6 | **LD:** 6+ | **OC:** 1
**Keywords:** Character, Epic Hero, Infantry, Imperium, Gravis, Adeptus Astartes, Iron Hands, Iron Father Ferrios

**Weapons:**
| Weapon | Type | Range | A | BS/WS | S | AP | D | Keywords |
|--------|------|-------|---|-------|---|----|---|----------|
| Gorgon's Wrath | Ranged | 36" | 3 | 2+ | 5 | -1 | 2 | Sustained Hits 2 |
| Harrowhand | Melee | Melee | 6 | 3+ | 7 | -2 | 2 | - |
| Medusan Manipuli | Melee | Melee | 2 | 3+ | 8 | -2 | 3 | Extra Attacks |

**Abilities:**

#### Rites of Tempering (Abilities)
- **Description:** While this model is leading a unit, models in that unit have the Feel No Pain 5+ ability

#### Iron Father (Abilities)
- **Description:** While this model is within 3" of one or more friendly Adeptus Astartes Vehicle units, it has the Lone Operative ability.

#### Master of the Forge (Abilities)
- **Description:** In your Command phase, select one friendly Adeptus Astartes Vehicle model within 3" of this model. That model regains up to 3 lost wounds and, until the start of your next Command phase, each time that Vehicle model makes an attack, add 1 to the Hit roll. You cannot select a unit for this ability that has already been selected for the Blessing of the Omnissiah ability this phase, and vice versa.

#### Leader (Abilities)
- **Description:** This model can be attached to the following units:
■ Aggressor Squad
■ Eradicator Squad
■ Heavy Intercessor Squad

#### Inspiring Commander (Abilities)
- **Description:** If you include this model in your army, until the end of the battle, non-Character models in Heavy
Intercessor Squad units from your army have an Objective Control characteristic of 3 while they are
not Battle-shocked.
